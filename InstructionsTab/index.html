How to Play Calculated Risk

Calculated Risk is a strategy-based card game that combines probability, logical reasoning, and decision-making. Compete against three computer-controlled opponents, use statistical clues to make informed decisions, and win the most tricks!

1. The Deck

  • The game uses 52 unique cards, numbered from 1 to 52.
  • There are four players: you and three computer-controlled opponents.
  • Each player receives 13 randomly distributed cards.
  • Higher-numbered cards are stronger than lower-numbered cards.
  • There are no suits or trump cards.

2. Playing a Trick

  • Each player selects one card from their hand to play.
  • All four players contribute one card to each trick.
  • The player who plays the highest-numbered card wins the trick.
  • The winner earns one point and leads the next trick.
  • A complete game consists of 13 tricks.

3. Probability Challenges

  • Throughout the game, you will encounter probability-based questions.
  • Use the clues provided about your opponents' cards to answer each question.
  • Apply concepts such as conditional probability, independent and dependent events, and probability distributions.
  • Remember that cards in your hand and cards already played are no longer unknown.
  • Use your calculations to make more informed decisions during gameplay.

4. Winning the Game

  • Your objective is to win more tricks than any other player.
  • Each trick won contributes one point to your final score.
  • After all 13 tricks have been played, the player with the highest score wins.
  • If multiple players share the highest score, the game ends in a tie.

5. Strategy Tips

  • Keep track of high-numbered cards that have already been played.
  • Use probability clues to estimate which cards your opponents might hold.
  • Avoid wasting your strongest cards when a weaker card could win the trick.
  • Think carefully before playing: every card can only be used once!

Ready to take the risk? Put your probability skills to the test and see if you can outsmart your opponents!
